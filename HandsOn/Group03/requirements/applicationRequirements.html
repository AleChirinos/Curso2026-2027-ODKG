Our idea for our application is to design a navigation app which warns the user about accident-prone locations in their route. The user will input their starting point and end point and what type of vehicle they are driving. Based on our data with locations of accidents in Madrid, the app will warn the user if their route goes through a location where a lot of accidents have happened in general and paying close attention to their type of vehicle. This way the user can plan on deviating from that location or at least be aware of the conditions. For this application we will use a routing API, which from what we understand openrouteservice offers this API free of charge. Then we will use predicting algorithms in order to predict the accidents.
