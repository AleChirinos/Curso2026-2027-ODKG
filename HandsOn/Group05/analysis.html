License analysis

Publisher: The publisher is the Ajuntament de València (València City Council). The official dataset page identifies "Ajuntament de València" as the organization responsible for publishing the dataset.

Rightsholder: The Ajuntament de València can also be considered the rightsholder for the published dataset. The data are made available under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence. The dataset itself identifies the Red Valenciana de Vigilancia y Control de la Contaminación Atmosférica as the source of the air-quality data, but the open-data portal attributes the dataset to the City Council.

License: The Valencia hourly air-quality dataset is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence. This allows the data to be reused, shared, and adapted, provided that appropriate attribution is given to the original source. The licence is explicitly stated on the official València Open Data dataset and CSV resource pages.

License used for the generated dataset: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), since our generated dataset/application uses and processes the dataset under the terms of this licence, with appropriate attribution to the Ajuntament de València.

Attribution: Our application is derived from the València Open Data hourly air quality dataset, published by the Ajuntament de València, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
